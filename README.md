SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development


##1. Problem Description
Agriculture is one of the most important sectors in India, providing employment and food resources to millions of people. However, farmers often face several challenges in selling their agricultural products at fair prices. The traditional agricultural supply chain involves multiple intermediaries, including wholesalers, agents, distributors and retailers. These intermediaries increase the final cost of products while reducing the amount of money received by farmers.

Small-scale farmers and Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs) often struggle to reach customers directly because of limited market access, lack of transportation facilities and insufficient information about market demand. As a result, farmers may be forced to sell their products at lower prices, even when the demand is high.


Consumers also face difficulties because fresh agricultural products are sometimes sold at higher prices due to transportation costs, storage expenses and multiple intermediaries. In addition, delays in transportation and poor supply chain management can lead to food wastage, especially for perishable products such as fruits and vegetables.

To overcome these challenges, a smart digital marketplace can be developed to connect farmers directly with consumers, retailers and bulk buyers. This platform can simplify agricultural trading, improve transparency and make the entire process more efficient.

Expected Solution
Create a digital marketplace that connects farmers and Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs) directly with consumers and bulk buyers.

Provide logistics support to ensure the smooth transportation and delivery of agricultural products.

Use Artificial Intelligence (AI) for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits
Better prices for farmers: Farmers can sell their products directly to buyers and receive better returns by reducing the involvement of intermediaries.

Lower prices for consumers: Consumers can purchase fresh agricultural products at affordable prices through direct connections with farmers.

Reduced supply chain inefficiencies: Improved logistics, demand forecasting and transportation planning can reduce delays, unnecessary costs and food wastage.

##2. Proposed Solution
The proposed solution is an AI-powered digital agricultural marketplace designed to improve the connection between farmers, consumers and bulk buyers. The platform provides a common digital space where farmers can list their agricultural products, buyers can explore available products, and transportation services can support the delivery process.

The main objective of this solution is to simplify agricultural trading and create a transparent marketplace that benefits both farmers and consumers.

The platform allows farmers to register and create their profiles by providing basic details such as their name, location, contact information and farming details. After registration, farmers can upload their available agricultural products with product names, quantities, prices, quality details and harvesting dates. This information helps buyers understand product availability and make purchasing decisions easily.

Consumers can access the platform to search for fresh fruits, vegetables, grains and other agricultural products. They can compare prices, check product availability and place orders according to their requirements. Bulk buyers, including supermarkets, restaurants, wholesalers and food processing companies, can purchase agricultural products in larger quantities directly from farmers or FPOs.

One of the important features of the proposed platform is its AI-based demand forecasting system. This feature analyses previous sales information, product availability and purchasing patterns to estimate future demand. It helps farmers understand which products may have higher demand and plan their agricultural production accordingly.

Another important feature is logistics support. The platform can connect farmers and buyers with transportation services to ensure products reach their destinations safely and on time. AI-based route optimization can identify suitable transportation routes by considering delivery locations, distances and traffic conditions. This can help reduce transportation delays and delivery expenses.

The platform also provides order tracking and notification facilities. Buyers can check their order status, while farmers can receive updates about new orders and payments. This improves communication between farmers and customers.

Overall, the proposed solution aims to create a reliable and user-friendly digital marketplace that makes agricultural trading easier, faster and more beneficial for everyone involved.

##3. Technical Approach
The project follows a systematic development approach to create a user-friendly and efficient agricultural marketplace. The system consists of different modules that work together to support product management, buyer registration, order processing, AI-based predictions and logistics management.

The platform can be developed as a web application or mobile application so that farmers and buyers can access its services easily.

The system includes the following major modules:

1. Farmer Registration Module

This module allows farmers and FPOs to register on the platform by entering their personal and agricultural details. Each farmer receives an individual account to manage products, view orders and update product availability.

2. Product Management Module

Farmers can add, update and remove agricultural products from their profiles. Product information includes product name, category, quantity, price, location and availability. This allows buyers to view updated product details.

3. Consumer and Buyer Module

Consumers and bulk buyers can create accounts, browse agricultural products, compare prices and place orders. The platform provides a simple search facility to help users find the products they need.

4. AI-Based Demand Forecasting Module

Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning techniques can analyse historical sales data, seasonal variations and purchasing patterns to predict future product demand. These predictions can help farmers make better decisions about production and product availability.

5. Logistics Management Module

This module supports transportation and delivery management by connecting farmers, buyers and delivery providers. It helps coordinate product collection, transportation and delivery.

6. AI-Based Route Optimization Module

The system can use location information, delivery destinations and route conditions to identify efficient transportation routes. This helps improve delivery planning and reduce unnecessary travel.

7. Order and Payment Management Module

This module manages order placement, order confirmation, payment records and delivery updates. It helps maintain transparency between farmers and buyers.

##4. Feasibility and Viability
The proposed AI-powered agricultural marketplace is feasible because it uses commonly available digital technologies and can be developed in different stages. The platform can initially focus on basic marketplace features and gradually introduce advanced AI-based services.

Technical Feasibility

The platform can be developed using existing web development technologies, databases and AI tools. Internet-enabled smartphones and computers allow farmers, buyers and administrators to access the system. Cloud-based services can also support data storage and application management.

Economic Feasibility

The project can be developed with a reasonable initial investment by using open-source technologies and affordable cloud services. By reducing unnecessary intermediaries and improving transportation planning, the platform has the potential to reduce supply chain expenses and improve the income opportunities of farmers.

Operational Feasibility

The system is designed with a simple interface so that users with basic digital knowledge can operate it. Farmers can upload products, buyers can place orders and delivery providers can manage transportation details through their respective accounts.

Potential Challenges

Limited internet connectivity in certain rural areas.

Lack of digital awareness among some farmers.

Difficulty in maintaining accurate product availability information.

Transportation and storage challenges for perishable agricultural products.

Maintaining trust between farmers, buyers and delivery providers.

Ensuring the accuracy of AI-based demand predictions.

Strategies to Overcome Challenges

Provide a simple and multilingual user interface.

Conduct basic digital awareness programmes for farmers.

Update product availability and pricing information regularly.

Establish partnerships with local transportation providers.

Introduce user ratings and feedback facilities.

Improve AI predictions using updated market and sales information.
